Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who this covers

"Learn From Leo pipeline" is a private tool used only by the operator of the Learn From Leo YouTube channel (@LearnFromLeoShorts) to upload that channel's own videos and read that channel's own statistics. It is not offered to the public and no other users sign in to it.

What data it accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool uses these Google scopes for the channel owner's account only:

  • YouTube upload – to upload the channel's own videos and set their titles, descriptions and publish times.
  • YouTube read-only – to list the channel's own videos and their public statistics.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only – to read the channel's own performance reports (views, watch time, retention).

How it is used and stored

Data is used solely to publish the channel's videos and to analyse what content performs well. It is stored only on the channel operator's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Viewers of the channel

The tool does not collect personal information about viewers. YouTube's own aggregated analytics are read only in summary form. Viewers' use of YouTube is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; locally stored data can then be deleted.
